Project · Windows

Daycast. A weather forecast for your PC time.

Daycast predicts how long you'll use the PC today, hour by hour, and which apps and sites you'll open, with a range of uncertainty. A light logger records apps and site domains in the background; the forecast comes from Mistral AI's Timestral model when, tested on the past week, it beats a simple baseline. Everything stays on the PC.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • Timestral
  • SQLite
  • pywebview
  • Windows UI Automation
